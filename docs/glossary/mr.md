# Marathi (mr) — glossary

The term evidence is recorded in `docs/reviews/mr.md`; this is the shared vocabulary. Besides
Apple's `mr-in` support articles, the Marathi translation files of Firefox, LibreOffice, VLC,
GIMP, GNOME Files and Transmission provide the product wording; a row records that wording where
the same word appears, and a `—` means the term is the neutral Marathi word, not a product's.
