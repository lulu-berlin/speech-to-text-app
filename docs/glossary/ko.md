# Korean (ko) — glossary

The term evidence is recorded in `docs/reviews/ko.md`; this is the shared vocabulary. Google's
Korean Meet help is fully translated and is the only product compared in this cycle, so a row
records its wording where the same word appears there; a `—` means the term is the neutral Korean
word, not a product's.
