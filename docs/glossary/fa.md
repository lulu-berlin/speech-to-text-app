# Persian (fa) — glossary

The term evidence is recorded in `docs/reviews/fa.md`; this is the shared vocabulary. The wording
recorded there comes from Google's Persian account and Chrome pages, Firefox, VLC, GNOME Files,
Nextcloud Files, Transmission, gedit and Midnight Commander; a `—` means no wording was recorded
for that term.

| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |

This glossary is the Persian layer; the same neutrality rule as the other locales applies (see
`docs/style-guides/fa.md`).
