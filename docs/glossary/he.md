## Gender and culture

- Hebrew verbs and nouns are gendered. The catalog prefers action nouns and plural forms that do
  not force the reader's gender: `שמירה`, `ביטול`, `מחיקה`, `הפעלה`, `השהיה`, `ניסיון נוסף`,
  `שינוי שיוך`, `מיזוג`; the direct address, where unavoidable, is plural (`בחרו`, `בדקו`,
  `גררו`) — the plural serves any gender. The app calls the speakers `קול`/`קולות` (voices),
  neutral and dot-free; the product's `דובר` is evidence only. The compared products' gendered
  forms stay in the review page as evidence.
- No idioms, no culture-specific examples; the product name `Speech-to-Text` stays untranslated.
- Technical nouns (`CPU`, `GPU`, the whisper language codes) stay as the products use them.
