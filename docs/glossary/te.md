# Telugu (te) — glossary

The term evidence is recorded in `docs/reviews/te.md`; this is the shared vocabulary. Google's
account page, the Telugu YouTube help and Apple's `te-in` support articles are fully Telugu, and
VLC, LibreOffice, GNOME Files, GNOME Terminal, gedit and Rhythmbox publish Telugu catalogs, so a
row records their wording where the same word appears there, the higher rank first; a `—` means
the term is the neutral Telugu word, not a product's.
