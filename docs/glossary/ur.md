# Urdu (ur) — glossary

The term evidence is recorded in `docs/reviews/ur.md`; this is the shared vocabulary. Google's
Chrome and Android Urdu strings and the Urdu account page, and Wikipedia's MediaWiki Urdu messages
(Apple's `ur-pk` pages redirect to English), are the products compared in this cycle; a row records
its wording where the same word appears there, and a `—` means no wording was recorded for it.
