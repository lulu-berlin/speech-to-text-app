## Gender and culture

- No generic masculine. The speaker terms are neutral: `Sprechende` (plural), `sprechende
  Person` (singular), `Anzahl der Sprechenden`, `Max. Sprechende`, `Trennung der Sprechenden`,
  `Erkennen der Sprechenden`. The compared products' GM forms (`Sprecher`, `Sprecheranzahl`,
  `Sprechertrennung`) stay in the review page as evidence; they are not adopted (user decision
  2026-11-12). The comparison is for idiomatic wording only: a non-gender-neutral or culturally
  insensitive term is never accepted, even when a product uses it.
- `du` address, imperatives; no idioms, no culture-specific examples; the product name
  `Speech-to-Text` stays untranslated.
- Technical nouns (`Threads`, `CPU`, `GPU`, `Upload`) stay as the products use them; they are
  not translated into rare German words.
