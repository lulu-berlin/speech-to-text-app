## Gender and culture

- No generic masculine. `الأصوات` (voices), `المداخلة` (a turn), the masdar labels; the
  products' `المتحدثون` is evidence only and never used. The comparison is for idiomatic
  wording only: a non-gender-neutral or culturally insensitive term is never accepted, even when
  a product uses it.
- Nominal/masdar address instead of the masculine imperative, so the reader is not gendered.
- Modern Standard Arabic; no dialect, no religious or culture-specific idiom; the product name
  `Speech-to-Text` stays untranslated.
