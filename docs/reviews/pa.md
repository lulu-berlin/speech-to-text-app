# Punjabi (pa) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a similar app or feature in
the product's own Punjabi UI, for idiomatic wording only; more popular products rank higher.
A dash in the Compared column means no comparison was found. A
non-gender-neutral or culturally insensitive term is never accepted, even when a compared product
uses it. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing
gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Punjabi UI: upload, transcripts, speaker identification, history, settings.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word) — Punjabi UI: meeting transcripts, connection states, settings.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Punjabi UI: playback, settings.

Only the products whose wording this record actually cites are listed.

Punjabi verbs and adjectives agree with the noun, never with the reader; the speaker noun
ਸਪੀਕਰ is a loanword used generically. The danda replaces an English full stop; other marks follow the English catalog, and Latin
words keep a half-width space. The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the
buttons show ਸੰਭਾਲੋ (S) and ਛੱਡੋ (D).

recorded yet. Audit gives the neutrality result, then any note.
