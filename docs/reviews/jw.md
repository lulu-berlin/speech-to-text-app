# Javanese (jw) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Javanese UI, for idiomatic wording only. The
Compared column names the product and the wording it uses; a dash means no comparison was found,
and a product name never appears without its wording. The mechanical parity check (keys,
placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google — the Search interface in Javanese (`google.com/webhp?hl=jv`,
   `google.com/preferences?hl=jv`): „Setelan“, „Basa“, partly mixed with Indonesian. Its account
   and help pages fall back (`support.google.com/?hl=jw` is Indonesian, `myaccount` English).
2. Microsoft (support) — `support.microsoft.com/jv-id` resolves to English; not compared.
3. Apple (support) — `support.apple.com/jv-in` resolves to English; not compared.

Javanese imperatives carry no grammatical gender, and no title or pronoun addresses the reader.
Every string was checked for cultural sensitivity: nothing loaded, no register drift, no calque
from English or Indonesian beyond the interface and technical loans the glossary lists. The
editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the buttons show Simpen
(S) and Buang (D).
