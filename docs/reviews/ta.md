# Tamil (ta) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Tamil UI, for idiomatic wording only. The Compared
column names the product and the wording it uses; a dash means no comparison was found, and a
product name never appears without its wording. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders,
ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products checked (popularity, 1 = most popular)

3. Apple (`support.apple.com/ta-in`) — the fully Tamil iPhone/iPad user guides: „அமைப்புகள்“,
   „சாதனம்“, „பதிவிறக்க“, „நீக்கு“, „தேர்ந்தெடு“, „கோப்புகளை“, „வரலாற்றைப் பார்வையிடுதல்“,
   „மீட்டமை“. Its wording is recorded where a comparable control or term was found. Google and
   Microsoft were not checked for Tamil wording in this cycle.

Tamil imperative forms carry no grammatical gender, and no title or pronoun addresses the
reader; `அவர்களின்` is the neutral plural. Every string was checked for cultural sensitivity:
nothing loaded, no Sanskritised register drift; the calques the copy review found (the verb-first
`queue.control.label`, `இங்கு நகர்த்து`, `மேம்பட்டவை`, `மிக மேலே`) were fixed in the same cycle.
The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the buttons show சேமி
(S) and நிராகரி (D).
