# Chinese (Simplified) (zh-Hans) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): every string is compared with a similar app or feature in
the product's own Simplified Chinese UI, for idiomatic wording only; more popular products rank
higher. A culturally insensitive term is never accepted, even when a compared product uses it.
The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Simplified Chinese UI: upload, search, transcripts.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word) — Simplified Chinese UI: meeting transcripts, connection states,
   settings.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Simplified Chinese UI: dictation, playback, editing, settings.
4. Zoom — Simplified Chinese UI: meeting, recording and transcript terms.
5. Otter.ai — Simplified Chinese UI: transcription and speakers.
6. Descript — Simplified Chinese UI: transcript editing, segments.
7. Amberscript — Simplified Chinese UI: transcription service.
8. GNOME Files (Files, formerly Nautilus) — Simplified Chinese UI: folders, opening files,
   format, path.
9. Midnight Commander — terminal file manager, Simplified Chinese gettext catalog: marking,
   moving, renaming, resuming, options.
10. GNU nano — terminal editor, Simplified Chinese gettext catalog: save, cancel, search, help,
    path, format, more.

Items 8–10 were added with the TUI strings: they are the established references for terminal key
hints and file-browser wording, and they follow the transcription products because they are not
transcription UIs.

Chinese has no grammatical gender, so the neutrality risks of the other locales do not arise:
the speaker noun is `说话人` (the voice's source, not a person), `他`/`她` never appear, and a task
or segment is `它` or an omitted subject. Punctuation is full-width (`，` `。` `？` `：`), the dash
is `——`, and Latin words keep a half-width space (`CPU 不可用`, `{filename} 已转写`).
The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the two buttons show
`保存（S）` and `放弃（D）`.

Verified wording: Google „转写“, „设置“, „设备“, „文件“, „停止“, „搜索“, „暂停“.

"Compared" gives the product whose terminology this string reuses; `—` means the string is a
phrase no compared product writes, and only the catalog's own terminology applies.
