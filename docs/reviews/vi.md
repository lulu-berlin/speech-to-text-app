# Vietnamese (vi) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Vietnamese UI, for idiomatic wording only; more
popular products rank higher. The Compared column names the product and the wording it uses; a
dash means no comparison was found, and a product name never appears without its wording. The
mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Vietnamese UI: uploads, transcripts, history, settings.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word) — Vietnamese UI: meeting transcripts, connection states, settings.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Vietnamese UI: playback.

Vietnamese has no grammatical gender; the speaker noun is người nói (the voice's source), and
no sentence can address the reader with a gendered form. Buttons are verb phrases. The editor's
shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the buttons show Lưu (L) and Bỏ (B).
