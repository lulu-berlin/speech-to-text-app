# Chinese (Traditional) (zh-Hant) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): every string is compared with a similar app or feature in
the product's own Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI, for idiomatic wording only; more popular
products rank higher. A culturally insensitive term is never accepted, even when a compared
product uses it. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the
standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: upload, search,
   transcripts.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word) — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: meeting transcripts, connection
   states, settings.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: dictation, playback, editing, settings.
4. Zoom — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: meeting, recording and transcript terms.
5. Otter.ai — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: transcription and speakers.
6. Descript — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: transcript editing, segments.
7. Amberscript — Traditional Chinese (Taiwan) UI: transcription service.

Chinese has no grammatical gender: the speaker noun is `說話者` (the voice's source, not a person),
no `他`/`她` pronoun appears, and a task or segment is `它` or an omitted subject. Taiwan's
vocabulary is used throughout — `佇列`, `設定`, `裝置`, `執行緒`, `檔案`, `使用者介面`, `預設`,
`轉錄`, `音訊` — never the Simplified catalog's terms. Punctuation is full-width (`，` `。` `？`
`：`), the dash is `——`, the quoted control uses `「…」`, and Latin words keep a half-width space
(`CPU 無法使用`, `{filename} 已轉錄`). The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter
per button, so the two buttons show `儲存（S）` and `捨棄（D）`.

Verified wording: Google „轉錄“, „設定“, „裝置“, „檔案“, „停止“, „搜尋“, „暫停“.

"Compared" gives the product whose terminology this string reuses; `—` means the string is a
phrase no compared product writes, and only the catalog's own terminology applies.
