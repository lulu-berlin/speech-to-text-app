# Portuguese (Brazil) (pt-BR) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): every string is compared with a similar app or feature in
the product's own Portuguese (Brazil) UI; more popular products rank higher; every string is
audited for gender neutrality and cultural sensitivity. The mechanical parity check (keys,
placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate; the user's review and approval are
the wording gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: upload, search, transcripts,
   voice typing.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word) — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: meeting transcripts, dictation, upload.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: dictation, playback, editing.
4. Zoom — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: meeting/recording/transcript terms.
5. Otter.ai — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: transcription, speakers, settings.
6. Descript — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: transcript editing, playback.
7. Amberscript — Portuguese (Brazil) UI: transcription service, upload, delivery.

Verified wording: Google „Transcrição“, „dispositivo“, „arquivo“, „Parar“; Microsoft
„transcrição“, „Baixar“, „Excluir“, „locutor“; Apple „Ajustes“, „Apagar“, „Reproduzir“.

Tone: `você` throughout, matching the English UI's direct address and the Brazilian products.
The speakers are `falantes` (common-gender `-e`), never `locutor`/`locutora`; the products'
gendered wordings stay in the table as evidence only. No idioms or culture-specific examples;
`Speech-to-Text`, file formats, `threads`, `CPU`, `GPU` and the whisper codes stay untranslated.

"Compared" gives the product and its Portuguese wording (or the closest feature); "Audit"
records the neutrality and idiom check.
