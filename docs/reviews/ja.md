# Japanese (ja) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): every string is compared with a similar app or feature in
the product's own Japanese UI, for idiomatic wording only; more popular products rank higher. A
culturally insensitive term is never accepted, even when a compared product uses it. The
mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google Workspace (Docs/Drive/Meet) — Japanese UI: upload, search, transcripts, player controls.
2. Microsoft (Teams/Word, Windows Terminal, Azure task states) — Japanese UI and Japanese docs: meeting transcripts, connection states, settings, task states, terminal settings.
3. Apple (macOS/iOS) — Japanese UI: dictation, playback, editing, Terminal.
4. VLC — official Japanese gettext catalog: playback controls, paths, filters, close, format.
5. GNOME Files (nautilus) — official Japanese gettext catalog: folders, open, rename, go up, search, back, undo, selection.
6. Midnight Commander — official Japanese gettext catalog: terminal menus for delete, move, up, quit, help, resume, retry, format.
7. GNOME Terminal — official Japanese gettext catalog: themes, reset, command, automatic.
8. gedit — official Japanese gettext catalog: undo, start and end of the document, pages, paste.

Only the products whose wording this record actually cites are listed.

Japanese has no grammatical gender; the speaker noun is `話者` (the voice's source), and no
sentence addresses the reader with a gendered form. Body sentences end with `。` (labels and queue lines mirror the English
punctuation); the full-width `？` takes no space after it, the em dash is set tight, and Latin
words and placeholders keep a half-width space where needed. The editor's shortcut check requires one
underlined letter per button, so the buttons show `保存 (S)` and `破棄 (D)`.

"Compared" gives the product whose terminology this string reuses; `—` means no comparison is
recorded yet. "Audit" gives the neutrality result, then any note.
