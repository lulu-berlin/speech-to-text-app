# Korean (ko) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Korean UI, for idiomatic wording only. The Compared
column names the product and the wording it uses; a dash means no comparison was found, and a
product name never appears without its wording. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders,
ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products checked (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google (Meet ko) — fully translated Korean help pages: „스크립트“, „스크립트 작성“, „설정“,
   „재설정“, „기기“, „파일“, „저장“, „취소“, „삭제“, „닫기“, „열기“, „계속“, „병합“. Its wording
   is recorded where a comparable control or term was found.
2. Microsoft (support) — not checked for Korean wording in this cycle.
3. Apple (support) — not checked for Korean wording in this cycle.

Korean marks no gender in the polite instruction form or the noun labels, and no pronoun or
title addresses the reader; placeholders that take a particle carry both forms. Every string was
checked for cultural sensitivity: nothing loaded, no Chinese-character register drift, no calque
from English. The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the
buttons show 저장 (S) and 버리기 (D).
