# Telugu (te) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Telugu UI, for idiomatic wording only. The Compared
column names the product and the wording it uses; a dash means no comparison was found, and a
product name never appears without its wording. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders,
ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

VLC, LibreOffice, GNOME Files, GNOME Terminal, gedit and Rhythmbox were added because their
published Telugu catalogs are the only Telugu wording for these controls; they are ranked by
popularity against the list. GNU nano's Translation Project index has no `te.po`, so it supplies
nothing. No product names the TUI's own queue or speaker screen, and Apple's transcript is a
podcast's, so those rows record the closest feature — a playlist's queue, a podcast's transcript,
a loudspeaker's wording — and the glossary's neutral term stands for the person. The key labels
themselves stay as the program binds them.

Telugu imperatives in the respectful plural mark no gender, and no title or pronoun addresses
the reader; sentences about the neuter `టాస్క్` agree in the neuter, and `వారి` is the neutral
plural. Every string was checked for cultural sensitivity: nothing loaded, no Hindi or English
calque, no register drift. The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per
button, so the buttons show సేవ్ చేయండి (S) and విస్మరించండి (D).
