# Indonesian (id) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Indonesian UI, for idiomatic wording only. The
Compared column names the product and the wording it uses; a dash means no comparison was found,
and a product name never appears without its wording. The mechanical parity check (keys,
placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google — the fully Indonesian account page (`myaccount.google.com/intro?hl=id`): „Setelan“,
   „perangkat“, „tugas“. Its wording is recorded where a comparable control or term was found.
   Microsoft and Apple were not compared in this cycle.

Indonesian verbs carry no grammatical gender, and no title or pronoun addresses the reader.
Every string was checked for cultural sensitivity: nothing loaded; the register and calque slips the copy review found (`punya`, `Tugas menunggu`,
`Perubahan tersimpan`) were fixed in the same cycle. The editor's shortcut check requires one underlined letter per button, so the
buttons show Simpan (S) and Buang (D).
