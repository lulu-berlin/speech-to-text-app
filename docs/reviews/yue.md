# Cantonese (yue) — translation review

Standard (user decision 2026-11-12): the record holds the comparison found per string with a
similar app or feature in the product's own Hong Kong Chinese UI, for idiomatic wording only;
more popular products rank higher. The Compared column names the product and the wording it
uses; a dash means no comparison was found, and a product name never appears without its
wording. The mechanical parity check (keys, placeholders, ICU, plural categories) is the
standing gate.

## Products compared (popularity, 1 = most popular)

1. Google (Meet zh-HK) — 轉錄稿 and 轉錄 for meeting transcripts.
2. Apple (zh-HK support and macOS/iOS) — 上載, 下載, 檔案, 設定.

The interface is written Chinese in Hong Kong usage, not colloquial Cantonese (see
`docs/style-guides/yue.md`). Chinese has no grammatical gender, no sentence can address the
reader with a gendered form, and `講者` names the voice's source. The editor's shortcut check
requires one underlined letter per button, so the buttons show 儲存（S）and 捨棄（D）.
